📖 VideoCreator Quick Start Guide

1. Import Photos & Music: Use the Media panel or File > Import to upload your photos and music track.
2. Customize Animation & Duration: Tap any photo clip to change its camera trajectory (Ken Burns, Zoom In/Out, Pan) or drag its edge to set duration.
3. ⚡ 1-Click Beat Sync: Open the Audio & Beats tab and tap Magic Beat Sync to automatically align your photos with music beats!
4. Apply Color Grading: Select from 8 cinematic LUT filters (Cinematic 35mm, Teal & Orange, Sunset Glow, Noir).
5. Export & Share: Click 🚀 Export MP4 to download your video in universal 1080p full HD format.
